Executive summary

The Auditbase engine conducted a comprehensive security review of the NovaVault Protocol, focusing on correctness, authorization, and externally observable behavior across the vault boundary. The review covered permissioning paths, state mutation ordering, oracle consumption, and asset flows through deposits and withdrawals.

One critical-severity defect was confirmed: the withdrawal path performs an external call before updating internal accounting, permitting classic re-entry and drainage of pooled funds. A proof-of-concept exploit was generated and validated in simulation. Remediation is a two-line reorder; we recommend blocking deployment until C-01 and H-01 are resolved.

Findings — 4
C-01 · CRITICAL

Reentrancy in withdraw() — state update after external call

Confidence 90% · Very likely

The function transfers ETH to msg.sender via a low-level call before decrementing the caller's share balance. A malicious receiver contract can re-enter withdraw() in its fallback and repeat the drain until the vault is empty. The generated PoC extracts the full pooled balance in a single transaction.

11function withdraw(uint256 amount) external {
12 require(shares[msg.sender] >= amount, "insufficient");
13 (bool ok, ) = msg.sender.call{value: amount}("");
14 require(ok, "transfer failed");
15 shares[msg.sender] -= amount;
16}

external interaction (L13) precedes effect (L15) — checks-effects-interactions violated

RECOMMENDATIONMove the share decrement above the external call, or guard the function with a reentrancy lock. Both fixes validated against the PoC.
H-01 · HIGH

Oracle price accepted without staleness or round validation

Confidence 84% · Likely

latestRoundData() is consumed without checking updatedAt or answeredInRound. During oracle outages the vault will price positions against stale data, enabling under-collateralized exits during volatility.

RECOMMENDATIONBound acceptable staleness (e.g. 30 minutes for this feed), verify round completeness, and revert on violation.
M-01 · MEDIUM

setGuardian() lacks access control

Confidence 96% · Very likely

Any address may replace the guardian and thereby control pause behavior. Intent inferred from documentation ("only the guardian pauses") conflicts with implementation — flagged by the adversarial layer, invisible to pattern matching.

RECOMMENDATIONRestrict to owner or current guardian via modifier.
L-01 · LOW

Floating pragma ^0.8.19

Confidence 99% · Certain

Contracts should be deployed with a pinned compiler version to rule out behavioral drift between builds.

RECOMMENDATIONPin to a tested release, e.g. 0.8.19 exactly.
