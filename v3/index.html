Executive summary

The Auditbase engine conducted a comprehensive security review of the NovaVault Protocol, focusing on correctness, authorization, and externally observable behavior across the vault boundary. One critical-severity defect was confirmed: the withdrawal path performs an external call before updating internal accounting, permitting classic re-entry and drainage of pooled funds. Remediation is a two-line reorder; we recommend blocking deployment until C-01 and H-01 are resolved.

Findings — 4
C-01 · Critical

Reentrancy in withdraw()

90% · very likely

The function transfers ETH to msg.sender via a low-level call before decrementing the caller's share balance. A malicious receiver can re-enter withdraw() in its fallback and repeat the drain until the vault is empty.

11function withdraw(uint256 amount) external {
13 (bool ok, ) = msg.sender.call{value: amount}("");
15 shares[msg.sender] -= amount;
16}
RecommendationMove the share decrement above the external call, or guard with a reentrancy lock. Both validated against the PoC.
H-01 · High

Oracle price accepted without staleness check

84% · likely

latestRoundData() is consumed without checking updatedAt or answeredInRound. During oracle outages the vault prices against stale data.

RecommendationBound acceptable staleness, verify round completeness, revert on violation.
M-01 · Medium

setGuardian() lacks access control

96% · very likely

Any address may replace the guardian and control pause behavior. Intent inferred from docs conflicts with implementation — flagged by the adversarial layer.

RecommendationRestrict to owner or current guardian via modifier.
L-01 · Low

Floating pragma ^0.8.19

99% · certain

Deploy with a pinned compiler version to rule out behavioral drift between builds.

RecommendationPin to a tested release, e.g. 0.8.19 exactly.
